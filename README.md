# Bruce

**Systems builder | Infrastructure | Automation | Technical operations**

My favorite work starts with, "This should exist," and ends with something deployed, tested, documented, and maintainable. I am especially interested in infrastructure, systems integration, technical operations, automation, and AI-enabled workflow roles.

## Home lab

My primary virtualization platform is Proxmox VE on a Dell Precision T7820 with dual Intel Xeon Gold 6130 processors, 256 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA RTX 3090 Ti. The lab gives me a place to deploy services, test network and recovery designs, and learn by operating real systems.

The public [Homelab Portfolio](https://github.com/whosebruce/homelab-portfolio) documents the engineering layer: segmented networks, virtualized compute, centralized storage, local and hosted AI workloads, role-separated Hermes agents, staged updates, monitoring, and recovery. It is deliberately sanitized so the portfolio demonstrates operating judgment without publishing live infrastructure details.

## Background

My military background started with four years in the U.S. Marine Corps as a 0431 Logistics/Embarkation Specialist. Since 2019, I have served in the Army National Guard as a 25B Information Technology Specialist. I am now transitioning to 35T, Military Intelligence Systems Maintainer/Integrator, a role focused on maintaining and integrating the technical systems that support military intelligence and S2 operations.

Those roles shape how I approach technical work: understand the mission, account for constraints, build for failure, and leave the system ready for the next person.
